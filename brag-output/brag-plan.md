# Brag Plan: HandoffLedger

## What is this app?
HandoffLedger is a verifiable knowledge handover and single-point-of-knowledge (SPOK) risk platform that turns scattered commits, chats, and task boards into verified Day-1 starter kits with zero leaked secrets.

## The angle
Engineering turnover causes costly brain drain when undocumented decisions, credentials, and workarounds walk out the door. HandoffLedger replaces messy wikis with deterministic citation verification, automated bottleneck detection, a 10-minute leaver exit interview, and a secret shield that guarantees passwords never leak into newcomer handovers.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A stark, high-contrast typography statement in deep obsidian:
"When an engineer leaves, their knowledge shouldn't leave with them."
A sudden pulse of emerald energy sparks into the central glowing 3D Knowledge Core.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Central Sculptural Knowledge Core with orbiting verifiable claim cards (`PostgreSQL 16 on port 5432: Verified in Git`).
- The Knowledge Bottleneck Heatmap showing single-author risk calculation ($C_n$).
- The Day-1 Starter Kit 4-pane command center: Gotchas & Workarounds, Important Accounts, Key Decisions, and Next Steps.
- The Secret Shield Canary Defense proving zero passwords or private keys leak to newcomers.

## Outro / punchline
"45% faster onboarding. 82% less reading. 0 leaked secrets. Never lose critical project knowledge."
Final brand lockup: **HandoffLedger**.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Scattered project activity ingested from raw Git commits and Discord chats.
2. **Key Action**: Bottleneck risk engine flags key-person dependencies and initiates a 10-minute yes/no gap review.
3. **Result**: Incoming team members get an instant 4-pane Day-1 Starter Kit with verified code citations and zero leaked secrets.
